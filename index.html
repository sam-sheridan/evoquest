EvoQuest

Design a species. Release 25. The world decides the rest.

Each creature has an energy bar. Food fills it; being big or fast drains it quicker. Once a creature is 15 seconds old, eating with a nearly full bar gives it one offspring with slightly varied traits. The more neighbours it has, the less likely it is to breed. Everyone lives at most 90 seconds.
